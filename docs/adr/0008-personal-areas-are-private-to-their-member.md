# A Personal Area is private to its Member, even from the Workspace Admin

A Member has to be able to write a Quick Note without first choosing where it belongs, so every Member gets a Personal Area where Quick Notes land. Only that Member sees it: it carries no Grants and no Share Links, and the Workspace Admin, who sees every other Area, does not see it. People only write raw notes into a place nobody else can read; to share something, the Member moves it to another Area, where the Grants of that Area apply.

When a Member is deactivated, their Personal Area stays as it is and comes back if they are reactivated. Only then may the Workspace Admin hand its content over to an ordinary Area, and only as a whole, so that what a departed colleague wrote is not lost.

## Considered Options

- **A Page without an Area.** Every Page sits in the tree of an Area, and Grants, search and export all rely on that. A Page outside any Area would need its own rule in each of them.
- **One shared inbox Area for everyone.** Needs nothing new, but every Member would read everyone else's unsorted notes.
- **A Personal Area the Workspace Admin can read.** Keeps "the Workspace Admin sees every Area" without exception, but takes away the reason to use it.

## Consequences

- A Personal Area is an Area in every other respect (tree, Folders, Versions, Trash), so moving a Quick Note out of it is an ordinary move.
- An Agent acting for a Member reaches that Member's Personal Area; a standalone Agent never does.
- Guests have no Personal Area and no Quick Notes.
- A Personal Area cannot be renamed, deleted or granted.
