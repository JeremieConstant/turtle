An open source Notion alternative for your team wiki

Notion is a polished all-in-one workspace with databases, templates and many integrations. Turtle does less on purpose. It is a self-hosted Markdown wiki where people and AI agents write docs together.

Short answer: is Turtle a good Notion alternative?

Yes, if you use Notion mainly as a team wiki and want to host it yourself under an open source licence.

No, if your team relies on databases, boards, templates, comments or guests. Turtle has none of these today and will never have databases. It is a focused wiki, not a workspace suite.

Why teams choose Turtle

Your wiki runs where your data should live

Turtle is one binary that stores data in SQLite plus a data directory. There is no database server and no external service.

You start it with docker compose up -d. For a backup, you stop Turtle and copy the data directory.

Open source without a paid tier

Turtle is AGPL-3.0 with no paid edition and no CLA. Every feature is in the repository, so you can audit and change the code.

AI agents that follow your permissions

Turtle has an MCP server at <your-address>/mcp. Claude and other MCP clients connect with OAuth. An agent never gets more access than the member who connected it, and its edits show up under that member's name, for example "Demo Member via Claude".

You can limit access to read-only or single areas, and admins can switch AI off workspace-wide.

Moving from Notion to Turtle

Notion exports pages as Markdown, and Turtle imports Markdown. That covers wiki pages. Databases export as CSV, and Turtle has no place for them. Test a few pages in the demo before you move everything.

When Notion is the better choice

Stay with Notion if you need databases, boards, timelines or the template gallery. Turtle will not add databases, because it only stores content that can be expressed as Markdown.

Notion also has comments, guests and public sharing, which Turtle plans for later releases. It has a polished interface, many integrations, and SAML SSO and audit logs on Enterprise.

Turtle is early. Until 1.0, data format and API can change between releases. If you want a mature managed service, Notion is the safer pick today.

Turtle vs Notion, answered

Is Turtle a free Notion alternative?

Self-hosting Turtle is free, with no feature limits. You pay for your server. A hosted version is announced, with no price or date yet.

Can I self-host Notion?

Notion does not list a self-hosted edition. Its Enterprise plan lets you pick a data region, such as Frankfurt for the EU.

Does Turtle have Notion-style databases?

No. Turtle supports headings, lists, checklists, tables, code, images and attachments. Databases are out of scope by design.

Can AI agents write in Turtle like in Notion?

Yes. Both offer MCP with OAuth and respect the connecting user's permissions. In Turtle, the server runs inside your own instance.
